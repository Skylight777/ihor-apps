From the developer’s App Store description.

The GLP-1 diary that stays on your phone. No account, no subscription, no ads.

GLP-1 Log keeps your weekly or daily shots in one place: which medicine, what dose, where you injected, and when the next one is due. Three taps and the shot is logged.

Log a shot in seconds

  • Medicine and dose are remembered from last time
  • Tap the spot on the body map: belly, thigh or upper arm, left or right
  • The app suggests the next spot so you rotate instead of guessing
  • Mark when you start a new pen and see how many doses are left

See your next shot

  • Countdown to shot day and a reminder on the day
  • A heads-up when your pen is running low

Track what matters

  • Weekly weight in kg or lb
  • Side effects with how strong they were: nausea, tiredness, stomach pain and more
  • Charts for weight, dose over time and side effects

For your doctor

  • Export a clean PDF report or a CSV file before your appointment

Works with

Semaglutide, tirzepatide, liraglutide, dulaglutide, oral GLP-1 tablets, or add your own medicine.

Private by design

Everything is stored on your iPhone. Lock the app with Face ID. Save a backup file when you change phones.

Simple pricing

The first 4 injections and 30 days are free. One purchase unlocks everything forever. No subscription.

Who it's for

• Anyone on a weekly or daily GLP-1 shot who wants one clear record
• People who keep forgetting which side they injected last time
• Anyone whose doctor asks: how is the weight going, any side effects?
Nothing to set up and no account: open the app on shot day and log it.

GLP-1 Log is a diary. It does not give medical advice and never suggests doses. Your prescriber decides your treatment.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you begin.

What does GLP-1 Log do?+

GLP-1 Log is a GLP-1 injection tracker app for iPhone. Keep a record of medicines, doses, injection sites, weight and side effects. See when your next shot is due.

Does GLP-1 Log require a subscription?+

No. The first 4 injections and 30 days are free. One purchase unlocks everything forever. No subscription.

Where is my data stored?+

Records and projects stay on your iPhone. No account or cloud server is required. Check the app’s backup and export options before changing devices or deleting it.

What iPhone software does it need?+

The current listing requires iOS 17.0 or later. Check the App Store for the latest compatibility and availability.
