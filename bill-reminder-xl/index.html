MADE FOR REAL LIFE

Organize recurring bills and due-date reminders.

From the developer’s App Store description.

Bills arrive on their own schedule. One always slips past you, and you find out when the late fee lands.

Put a bill in once: name, amount, the date it is due, how often it repeats. After that the phone keeps the dates.

Reminders before the date, not after

A notification comes on the due date. Add earlier ones if you need a few days to move money. You set the time of day once and every bill uses it.

One tap when you pay

Tap the checkmark. The payment drops into History and the bill rolls forward to its next date by itself. A one time bill goes to the archive instead. Nothing gets deleted.

The month in one screen

Upcoming splits your bills into overdue, due this week, and later. At the top: how much is still to pay this month, and how much is already paid.

Stays on this iphone

No account, no cloud, no ads, no analytics. Rent, power, insurance, loan payments: none of it leaves the phone. Works with no internet at all.

45 languages and your currency

Both are in Settings, along with the time of day your reminders arrive.

One price, paid once

Three bills with their reminders, one-tap payments and 30 days of history are free and stay free. One purchase lifts the limit for good: unlimited bills and full payment history, with no subscription and nothing to renew. Whatever you have already entered is yours either way.
