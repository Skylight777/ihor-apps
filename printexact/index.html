MADE FOR REAL LIFE

Print a photo at an exact size from iPhone.

From the developer’s App Store description.

Print a photo at the exact size you asked for. Type 10 x 15 cm, 4 x 6 in, or any width and height you like — PrintExact places it on the paper at that exact size and prints it that way.

No more "why is my 5 x 7 cropped in half?" and no more prints that come out a few millimetres short.

Exact size, not a guess

  • Type any width and height in mm, cm or inches
  • Presets: 10 x 15, 13 x 18, 20 x 30, 4 x 6, 5 x 7, 8 x 10 and document sizes
  • Lock the proportions, or set both sides freely
  • A ruler along the sheet shows how big it really is

The whole picture, not a crop

  • Fit — the whole photo inside your size, nothing cut off
  • Fill — your size filled edge to edge
  • Stretch — exactly the numbers you typed
  • Drag the frame to choose what stays in the picture

Big posters from ordinary sheets

  • Turn one photo into a poster spread over several A4 or Letter sheets
  • 50 x 70 cm, 70 x 100 cm, A2, A1, A0, or a size of your own
  • Overlap for gluing, cut marks and a small map of which sheet goes where
  • You see how many sheets it will take before you print anything

Print or export

  • AirPrint straight from your iPhone
  • Or export PDF, JPEG or PNG at the exact size and print it anywhere
  • Several copies on one sheet, or fill the sheet with copies automatically

When the printer is off by a millimetre

Some printers quietly scale everything down. Print the calibration page once, measure the line with a ruler, type what you measured — and every print after that comes out true to size.

Projects

Pages are saved as projects. Open one tomorrow, change the size, print it again.

Private and offline

Everything happens on your iPhone. No account, no cloud, no ads, no subscription, no internet needed.

Simple pricing

The first three prints or exports are free. One purchase unlocks everything forever.

PrintExact is for anyone who needs the print to be the size they asked for: photos for frames and albums, document photos, sewing and craft templates, model plans, stencils and wall art.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you begin.

Does PrintExact require a subscription?+

No. The first three prints or exports are free. One purchase unlocks everything forever.

Where is my data stored?+

Records and projects stay on your iPhone. No account or cloud server is required. Check the app’s backup and export options before changing devices or deleting it.

What iPhone software does it need?+

The current listing requires iOS 17.0 or later. Check the App Store for the latest compatibility and availability.
