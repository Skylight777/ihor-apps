MADE FOR REAL LIFE

Keep cholesterol lab results in a large-print journal.

From the developer’s App Store description.

The cholesterol log with big numbers. Your lipid panel results in one place: no account, no subscription, no ads.

Cholesterol Log XL is made for people who get a blood test a few times a year and want to keep the numbers without squinting. Copy Total, LDL, HDL and Triglycerides from your lab sheet, and the app keeps every result in a clean, large-print list.

Log a panel in seconds

  • Four big fields, one big wheel: tap a value, turn the wheel, done
  • Total is enough. Leave LDL, HDL or Triglycerides at “Not measured” if your sheet does not show them
  • Add the test date and a short note: lab name, fasting
  • Your last result is remembered, so the next entry takes a few turns of the wheel

See every result

  • Your newest panel on top, printed like a lab slip with big numbers
  • Older results below, newest first
  • TC/HDL shown as a plain number, exactly as many lab sheets print it
  • Tap any result to fix a number or delete it

For your doctor

  • Share a clean text report of all your results
  • Export a CSV file for a spreadsheet or your clinic
  • Reports use the units you chose

Your units

Pick mg/dL or mmol/L, whichever is printed on your lab sheet. Cholesterol and triglycerides are converted with their own correct factors. Switch anytime in Settings.

Next test reminder

Choose 1, 3, 6 or 12 months after your last result and get a reminder when it is time for the next blood test.

Private by design

Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no cloud, no analytics, no tracking. Delete the app and the data goes with it, so export a report first.

Simple pricing

The last three results are always free. One purchase unlocks your full history and the doctor's report forever. No subscription.

Who it's for

  • Anyone who checks cholesterol regularly and wants all the numbers in one place
  • Older eyes: large print, high contrast, big buttons
  • Anyone whose doctor asks “what were your numbers last time?”

Cholesterol Log XL is a journal. It does not test your blood, does not interpret your results and does not give medical advice. Always talk to your doctor about your health.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you begin.

Does Cholesterol Log XL require a subscription?+

No. The last three results are always free. One purchase unlocks your full history and the doctor's report forever. No subscription.

Where is my data stored?+

Records and projects stay on your iPhone. No account or cloud server is required. Check the app’s backup and export options before changing devices or deleting it.

What iPhone software does it need?+

The current listing requires iOS 17.0 or later. Check the App Store for the latest compatibility and availability.
