From the developer’s App Store description.

Hot flash tracker and menopause symptom log. Every hot flash, with the time it actually happened. Pay once, no subscription.

Most menopause apps only let you say "I had hot flashes today". That is not what your doctor needs to see. Meno Log saves each flash as its own moment: tap the circle, choose how strong it was, done. Two taps, exact time — and you can correct the time later for the one that woke you at three in the morning.

Logged in two taps

  • One big button for a hot flash — the time is saved for you
  • Nine more symptoms: night sweats, sleep, mood, brain fog, headache, fatigue, palpitations, joint pain, plus your own
  • Three levels of intensity, an optional note, full edit and delete

Your day and your night

History shows every day with flashes split into daytime and night, so the pattern is visible at a glance.

A report in a minute

Two weeks, one month or three months — share it as text or as a CSV file your doctor can open in any spreadsheet.

No account. no cloud.

No sign-up, no server, no ads, no analytics. Everything stays on this iPhone.

One payment, not a subscription

Logging is never limited. Your first 14 days open everything; after that, today and your last 7 days stay free forever. One purchase unlocks your full history and doctor reports forever.

Meno Log is a personal journal. It does not diagnose, does not interpret your entries and does not give medical or treatment advice. For anything about your health, talk to your doctor.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you begin.

Does Meno Log require a subscription?+

No. Logging is never limited. Your first 14 days open everything; after that, today and your last 7 days stay free forever. One purchase unlocks your full history and doctor reports forever.

Where is my data stored?+

Records and projects stay on your iPhone. No account or cloud server is required. Check the app’s backup and export options before changing devices or deleting it.

What iPhone software does it need?+

The current listing requires iOS 17.0 or later. Check the App Store for the latest compatibility and availability.
