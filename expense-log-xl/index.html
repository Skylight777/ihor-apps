Track what you spend — in seconds, on your phone, without a subscription.

Expense Log XL is a manual expense tracker for people who want their money in their own hands: type the amount, tap a category, done. No bank logins, no accounts, no cloud, no ads.

An expense in 3 seconds

  • A big numpad and big category buttons — one tap saves the expense
  • 10 ready-made categories, or build your own with your own icons
  • A note or a different date only when you actually need one

See where the money goes

  • This month's total at a glance
  • Every category with its share, as bars and percentages
  • Swipe left or right to walk through your months
  • Search your notes

Csv export that actually works

Date, Amount, Currency, Category, Note — a clean UTF-8 file with BOM that opens correctly in Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets. Commas and quotation marks inside your notes stay intact instead of breaking the columns.

Private by design

Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no server, no cloud sync, no analytics, no advertising. Your expenses are nobody else's business.

Honest pricing

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you begin.

What does Expense Log XL do?+

Expense Log XL is a simple expense tracker app with no subscription for iPhone. Enter an amount. Pick a category. Keep a clear monthly spending record without connecting your bank.

Does Expense Log XL require a subscription?+

No. Adding expenses is always free and never limited. Your first 14 days open everything. After that, the free version keeps this month in full, plus backup. One purchase unlocks every past month, CSV export of any period and one-tap recurring expenses. Forever, with no subscription.

Where is my data stored?+

Records and projects stay on your iPhone. No account or cloud server is required. Check the app’s backup and export options before changing devices or deleting it.

What iPhone software does it need?+

The current listing requires iOS 17.0 or later. Check the App Store for the latest compatibility and availability.
