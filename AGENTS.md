Target: GitHub Pages at https://skylight777.github.io/ihor-apps/.
No dependencies, accounts, analytics, backend or runtime AI.
Source: data/apps.json (public US Apple catalog snapshot, 2026-10-26), editorial copy in scripts/build.py.
Build: python3 scripts/build.py. Preview: python3 -m http.server 8765.
Keep descriptions factual; do not invent app features, pricing, testimonials or medical claims.
App Store Connect is READ ONLY; never change app records or settings.
Keep app policies in the existing igor-apps-legal repository; do not modify that repository without instruction.
Verify real browser interactions and responsive layout before committing.
Update docs/SESSION_PROGRESS.md and append docs/DEVLOG.md after each meaningful stage.
