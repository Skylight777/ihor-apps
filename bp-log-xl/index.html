GOOD TO KNOW

Before you begin.

What does BP Log XL do?+

BP Log XL is a large-print blood pressure log app for iPhone. A blood pressure journal with large numbers. Record systolic, diastolic and pulse, then share your history.

Does BP Log XL require a subscription?+

No. Your first 14 days open everything. After that, logging, your last 7 days and backup stay free forever. One purchase unlocks your full history, 30- and 90-day trends and CSV export, forever. No subscription. If you already unlocked the app, nothing changes for you.

Where is my data stored?+

Records and projects stay on your iPhone. No account or cloud server is required. Check the app’s backup and export options before changing devices or deleting it.

What iPhone software does it need?+

The current listing requires iOS 17.0 or later. Check the App Store for the latest compatibility and availability.
