MADE FOR REAL LIFE

Track children’s cash allowance and chores without a bank card.

From the developer’s App Store description.

Pocket Money is the allowance tracker for parents who pay their kids in cash. Log weekly allowance, chores with a reward, gifts and spending, and see every child's balance at a glance. No bank card, no account, no internet needed.

What it does

  • One tap to add allowance, a chore reward or a gift
  • Weekly allowance is credited automatically, even after a week away from the app
  • Chores with a price: tap Done and the money lands in the child's balance
  • Savings goals with a progress bar for the bike, the game or the trip
  • A clear history for every child: who got what, when and why
  • Every child gets a card with balance, saved amount and weekly allowance

Why parents like it

Cash is still the easiest way to teach kids about money, but paper notes get lost and nobody remembers who owes what. Pocket Money keeps the ledger so the pocket money itself can stay in the piggy bank. Kids see their balance grow, learn that chores earn money and that saving takes time.

Private by design

Everything stays on your iPhone. No bank card, no account, no cloud, no ads, no analytics.

Simple price

The first 14 days open everything. After that, the Notebook is free forever: one child, one savings goal, up to 3 chores, entries by hand and your last 30 days. Logging money is never blocked. One family purchase unlocks every child and every goal, automatic pocket money and full history with CSV export. No subscription.

Works everywhere

Choose any currency and any of 34 languages, independent of your iPhone settings. Light and dark mode.

Pocket Money is a private ledger for cash. It is not a bank and does not move real money.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you begin.

Does Pocket Money require a subscription?+

No. The first 14 days open everything. After that, the Notebook is free forever: one child, one savings goal, up to 3 chores, entries by hand and your last 30 days. Logging money is never blocked. One family purchase unlocks every child and every goal, automatic pocket money and full history with CSV export. No subscription.

Where is my data stored?+

Records and projects stay on your iPhone. No account or cloud server is required. Check the app’s backup and export options before changing devices or deleting it.

What iPhone software does it need?+

The current listing requires iOS 17.0 or later. Check the App Store for the latest compatibility and availability.
