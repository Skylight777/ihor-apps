From the developer’s App Store description.

The blood sugar journal made for seniors, and for everyone who hates squinting.

Sugar Log XL does one thing well: it keeps your glucose readings in giant, readable numbers. Log a reading in seconds with one big wheel, tag it (fasting, before or after a meal, bedtime), and show your doctor a clean history.

What you can log

  • Fasting glucose in the morning
  • Readings before and after meals
  • Bedtime readings
  • Any value from any glucose meter: the app is a diary, not a device

Who it is for

  • People with type 2 or type 1 diabetes who test at home
  • Prediabetes and gestational diabetes, when the doctor asks for a log
  • Sons and daughters helping a parent keep their numbers in order
  • Anyone replacing a paper logbook with something that fits in a pocket

Day and night

Light and dark mode with the same giant numbers. Readable on the nightstand at 6 a.m. and in the kitchen at noon.

Made to be easy

  • Giant numbers and big buttons on every screen
  • Log a reading in seconds: the wheel remembers your last value
  • mg/dL or mmol/L, match your meter in one tap
  • Clear history, newest first
  • Backup and restore all your readings from Settings, free

For your doctor

Export every reading as a simple CSV report your doctor can read at a glance.

Private by design

Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no cloud, no analytics, no ads.

Simple pricing

Your first 14 days open everything. After that, logging, your last 7 days and backup stay free forever. One purchase unlocks your full history and the doctor report, forever. No subscription. If you already unlocked the app, nothing changes for you.

Sugar Log XL is a journal. It does not measure blood sugar and does not give medical advice.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you begin.

What does Sugar Log XL do?+

Sugar Log XL is a large-print blood sugar log app for iPhone. Log blood sugar in large, readable numbers. Tag readings around meals and keep a history for your doctor.

Does Sugar Log XL require a subscription?+

No. Your first 14 days open everything. After that, logging, your last 7 days and backup stay free forever. One purchase unlocks your full history and the doctor report, forever. No subscription. If you already unlocked the app, nothing changes for you.

Where is my data stored?+

Records and projects stay on your iPhone. No account or cloud server is required. Check the app’s backup and export options before changing devices or deleting it.

What iPhone software does it need?+

The current listing requires iOS 17.0 or later. Check the App Store for the latest compatibility and availability.
