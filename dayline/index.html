Keep a one-line journal without a subscription.

From the developer’s App Store description.

DayLine is a one line a day journal and 5 year diary: write one sentence about your day in 20 seconds, and next year on the same date it shows you what you wrote. Writing is free every day with no limits, one purchase unlocks your whole journal, no subscription. Works offline in 45 languages.

DayLine is the journal you'll actually keep. Write a single line about your day, and next year, on the same date, it shows you what you wrote. Your years stack up, one honest sentence at a time.

Why it works

  • One line takes 20 seconds, so the habit survives busy days
  • "On this day" brings back what you wrote a year ago, and the year before that
  • A gentle reminder at the time you choose. If you already wrote today, it stays quiet

On this day

Open the app on any date and see the same date from your past years right under today's line. A birthday, a first day at work, an ordinary Tuesday you would have forgotten. This is what a paper five year diary does, without carrying the book around.

Calendar and timeline

The calendar shows which days have a line, month by month. The timeline is your journal as one long scroll. Tap any day to read it again.

Widget

Put today's line on your Home Screen. The medium widget also shows what you wrote one year ago.

Who it is for

  • You tried journaling and quit because a blank page asked too much
  • You want a 5 year diary that fits in your pocket
  • You keep a gratitude journal and one good thing a day is enough
  • You have one minute, not twenty

Private by design

Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no cloud, no analytics, no ads. Lock the app with Face ID. Back up and restore your journal anytime.

Simple pricing

Free forever: write every day with no limits, your last 30 days, On this day, reminders, the widget and backup. One purchase unlocks your whole journal, every year, plus export as text. No subscription.

DayLine speaks 45 languages.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you begin.

What does DayLine do?+

DayLine is a one-line-a-day journal app for iPhone. One line a day. The same date, year after year. A private journal for the moments you want to keep.

Does DayLine require a subscription?+

No. Free forever: write every day with no limits, your last 30 days, On this day, reminders, the widget and backup. One purchase unlocks your whole journal, every year, plus export as text. No subscription.

Where is my data stored?+

Records and projects stay on your iPhone. No account or cloud server is required. Check the app’s backup and export options before changing devices or deleting it.

What iPhone software does it need?+

The current listing requires iOS 17.0 or later. Check the App Store for the latest compatibility and availability.
