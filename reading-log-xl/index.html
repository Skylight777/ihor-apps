MADE FOR REAL LIFE

Track reading minutes and make a school reading report.

From the developer’s App Store description.

Reading Log XL is a reading log and book tracker: it records reading minutes, pages and books, and makes a one page PDF reading report for the teacher. Pay once, no subscription.

One button a day

Tap "I read today", pick the minutes, save. A built-in timer counts a reading session for you if you want it to.

Your bookshelf

Keep three shelves — reading now, finished, want to read. Title, author, page count, a coloured spine instead of a cover. No sign-up, no social feed, no reviews to scroll.

A report for school

Make a one-page PDF for a teacher or a parent: days read, minutes, pages and the books behind them. Pick 7 days, 30 days, a year or everything.

Goals and streaks

Set a daily goal in minutes and watch the streak build. Weekly and monthly totals show whether the habit is holding.

Private by default

Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no cloud, no ads, no analytics. Works on a plane.

Simple price

The free version always saves everything you log and shows the last 30 days plus three books at a time. One purchase unlocks the full history, unlimited books and PDF reports forever. No subscription.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you begin.

Does Reading Log XL require a subscription?+

No. The free version always saves everything you log and shows the last 30 days plus three books at a time. One purchase unlocks the full history, unlimited books and PDF reports forever. No subscription.

Where is my data stored?+

Records and projects stay on your iPhone. No account or cloud server is required. Check the app’s backup and export options before changing devices or deleting it.

What iPhone software does it need?+

The current listing requires iOS 17.0 or later. Check the App Store for the latest compatibility and availability.
