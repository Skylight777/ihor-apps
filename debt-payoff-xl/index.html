From the developer’s App Store description.

Debt Payoff XL is a debt snowball and avalanche planner: enter your credit cards, car loan and student loan once and see the month you will be debt free, with the interest each order costs. Pay once, no subscription: the first 3 debts are free forever and one purchase unlocks the rest. Works offline in 45 languages.

Splitting your spare money across everything feels fair, and it is exactly why nothing ever closes.

Enter each debt once: name, balance, annual rate, minimum payment. From there the app works out the order and the date.

Snowball or avalanche

Smallest balance first, or highest rate first. The Plan screen runs both and shows what each order costs you in interest. Pick the one you will actually stick with.

The date you are working toward

The plan is a month by month simulation of your own numbers. Raise the extra payment a little and the finish date moves while you watch.

One debt at a time

Minimums go to everything, and whatever is left goes into a single debt until it is gone. That debt then hands its whole payment, minimum included, to the next one in line. Your monthly total never changes, it just lands on one target.

When the minimum is too small

If the minimum does not cover the monthly interest, that balance grows instead of shrinking. The app says so on the debt itself instead of drawing a nicer chart.

Log a payment and watch it drop

Tap the debt, enter what you paid. The balance falls, History keeps the record, and the chart shows your total debt going down month by month.

Reminders

Each debt has its own day of the month. The time of day is set once and used by all of them.

Stays on this iphone

No account, no cloud, no ads, no analytics. Balances and rates do not leave the phone. It works with no internet at all.

45 languages and your currency

Both are in Settings, and only the symbol changes. Nothing is converted.

One price, paid once

Your first three debts are free for as long as you want them. One purchase lifts the limit for good, with no subscription and nothing to renew. Whatever you have entered is yours either way.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you begin.

Does Debt Payoff XL require a subscription?+

No. Your first three debts are free for as long as you want them. One purchase lifts the limit for good, with no subscription and nothing to renew. Whatever you have entered is yours either way.

Where is my data stored?+

Records and projects stay on your iPhone. No account or cloud server is required. Check the app’s backup and export options before changing devices or deleting it.

What iPhone software does it need?+

The current listing requires iOS 17.0 or later. Check the App Store for the latest compatibility and availability.
